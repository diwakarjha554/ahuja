# Ahuja PA World - Audience and Buyer Guide

Ahuja PA World (ahujapaworld.com) is a Delhi-based **authorised dealer** of Ahuja Radios public-address equipment. We are not the manufacturer; our value is availability, correct product selection, GST billing, and local support. Currency is INR only. The two phone lines on the site are **011-47090169** and **011-41450169**, and WhatsApp plus a phone call are the primary ways this audience reaches us.

The catalogue is about 50 products spanning speakers (ceiling, wall, outdoor, line array, portable, Bluetooth, neckband), power and mixer amplifiers, mixers, wired and wireless microphones, megaphones, counter communication systems, an audio interface, and accessories. Prices run from roughly INR 650 (AUD-54 wired mic) to INR 68,826 (ALX-11000 line array).

The buyer is almost always solving a practical problem: "my hall needs sound", "my shop needs announcements", "my teacher's voice does not carry", "customers cannot hear us across the counter". They are price-aware, warranty-conscious, and want a human to confirm they are ordering the right thing before they pay.

## Personas

### 1. Schools, colleges and coaching institutes
- **What they buy:** Neckband PA systems (NBA-15, NBA-20DP, NBA-30WL) for individual teachers; ceiling speakers (CSX, CSD series) and wall speakers (WSX) wired to a mixer amplifier (DPA-370/570M/770M) for corridors, assembly halls and classrooms; wired mics (AUD-54, AUD-59XLR) and a wireless mic (AWM-495V1/V2) for the stage; megaphones (AM series) for sports days and fire drills.
- **How they decide:** Budget sanctioned per term, bulk quantities (10-50 ceiling speakers at a time), need a proper GST invoice for the accounts department, want a single quote covering amplifier + speakers + cable + mics. Warranty and "will it be loud enough for our hall" matter more than brand prestige.
- **Channel:** Office staff on desktop for the quote and paperwork; principal or admin on mobile and WhatsApp for approvals and photos of the installed system.
- **Objections:** "Too expensive for our grant", "we need it installed, not just delivered", "can you match last year's price", "is on-site service available".

### 2. Offices, corporates, factories and warehouses
- **What they buy:** Ceiling speakers (CSX-3081T/5081T/6101T) for reception and open-plan floors; wall speakers (WSX) and outdoor speakers (OSX) for factory sheds and gates; a 100 V-line power or mixer amplifier (APA-240, DPA-570M); AI-202 audio interface for recording/streaming in meeting rooms.
- **How they decide:** Facility or admin manager wants reliability, low maintenance, and a clean look that blends with interiors. Approved vendor, GST invoice, and sometimes a purchase order. Quantity and 100 V-line wattage planning matter.
- **Channel:** Desktop for procurement, phone call to confirm stock and delivery timeline, WhatsApp to share floor plans.
- **Objections:** "Need delivery across multiple branches", "wattage matching for a long speaker run", "AMC/service support", "payment terms".

### 3. Places of worship - temples, gurdwaras, mosques, churches
- **What they buy:** Higher-power mixer amplifiers (DPA-770M, UBA-500DP) and power amplifiers (BR-250M); horn/outdoor speakers (OSX-666T/999T, SP-5251T sound projector) for courtyards; wall and ceiling speakers for prayer halls; wireless mics (AWM-495V1/V2) and the ALX-11000 line array or portable active speakers (XPA, ABA, VIVA) for large gatherings and festivals.
- **How they decide:** Committee decision, trust and word of mouth carry heavy weight, coverage over a large/open area is the priority, often a festival or event deadline. Durability for continuous daily use and easy operation by volunteers.
- **Channel:** Strongly phone and WhatsApp driven, often in Hindi or regional language; photos/videos of the venue shared to get a recommendation.
- **Objections:** "Will the sound reach the whole courtyard", "it must run for hours daily", "simple to operate", "price for the committee".

### 4. Hospitals and banks (counter communication and paging)
- **What they buy:** Counter communication systems (CCS-2200, CCS-2300) for pharmacy windows, enquiry, ticket and teller counters behind glass; ceiling/wall speakers for paging and waiting areas; gooseneck and wired mics.
- **How they decide:** Clear two-way speech across a glass barrier is the core requirement; hygiene (hands-free), reliability, and a compliant GST invoice for institutional accounts. Often tendered or quoted per counter in multiples.
- **Channel:** Desktop and email for procurement, phone to confirm compatibility and counts.
- **Objections:** "Does it work through our specific glass/aperture", "noise cancellation", "per-counter pricing", "service turnaround".

### 5. Event managers, tent houses, DJs and rental operators
- **What they buy:** Portable active speakers (XPA-1520DP/3010DP, ABA-4000/5000, VIVA-600), line array (ALX-11000), battery/Bluetooth trolley speakers (BTA-550/660M/880), high-power amplifiers (UBA-500DP), mixers (PMX-1032FX, PROMIX-1442, FMX-106DP) and wireless mics.
- **How they decide:** Return on rental investment, loudness and coverage per rupee, portability and ruggedness for repeated transport, quick setup. They buy in sets and re-buy consumables (mics, cables). Price and immediate availability beat everything.
- **Channel:** Mobile-first, WhatsApp for fast back-and-forth on price and stock, phone to close the deal.
- **Objections:** "Best price for cash", "stock ready today", "how many people will it cover", "spare parts and repair".

### 6. AV system integrators, contractors and electricians
- **What they buy:** Across the range in volume for client projects: ceiling/wall/outdoor speakers, 100 V-line amplifiers, volume controls (AT-06), brackets (AWB-30) and stands (BMS-101), connectors and cable.
- **How they decide:** Dealer pricing/margins, consistent stock, accurate spec sheets for their BOQ, amplifier-to-speaker wattage calculations, and dependable lead times. They are repeat professional buyers who value a vendor who never misquotes.
- **Channel:** Desktop for BOQs and quotes, phone and WhatsApp for day-to-day ordering.
- **Objections:** "Trade/volume pricing", "will you hold stock for a project", "data sheets and 100 V tap tables", "credit terms".

### 7. Government, PSU and institutional procurement
- **What they buy:** Public-address systems for offices, auditoriums, railway/bus counters, panchayat and municipal use; megaphones for disaster management and police; counter systems.
- **How they decide:** Tender and quotation process, authorised-dealer proof, GST and sometimes GeM-style compliance, make-in-India preference (Ahuja is Indian-made), warranty and documentation. Lowest compliant quote often wins.
- **Channel:** Email and desktop for formal quotes; phone to clarify specs.
- **Objections:** "Are you an authorised dealer", "documentation and warranty paperwork", "delivery to a government address", "rate contract".

### 8. Shops, showrooms, restaurants and hotels
- **What they buy:** A few ceiling or wall speakers (CSX, WSX) with a compact mixer amplifier (DPA-370, FMX-106DP) for background music and announcements; a volume control (AT-06); occasionally a Bluetooth trolley speaker for promotions.
- **How they decide:** Looks that suit the interior, simple operation, modest budget, quick delivery. Owner decides directly.
- **Channel:** Mobile and WhatsApp, often asking for "a complete set for my shop".
- **Objections:** "Give me the full set price", "easy to use", "will it look good", "quick delivery".

### 9. Individuals - home and small events
- **What they buy:** Entry wired mics (AUD-54), a small amplifier (DPA-370) or a Bluetooth trolley speaker (BTA-550), a neckband system for a yoga/fitness instructor, a megaphone for a society event.
- **How they decide:** Price first, reviews and reputation, whether it is genuine Ahuja, and whether support exists if it fails. Lowest commitment buyer.
- **Channel:** Mobile-first, searches on Google in Hinglish, WhatsApp to ask "original hai kya" and price.
- **Objections:** "Is it original Ahuja", "cheapest price", "home delivery with warranty", "can I return it".

## What the storefront must make easy

- **Bulk quote request:** A prominent "Request a quote" / "Get bulk price" action on every product and category, capturing quantity, pincode and GST number, routed to WhatsApp or the sales desk. Most high-value orders close by quote, not cart checkout.
- **Call and WhatsApp everywhere:** Sticky click-to-call (011-47090169, 011-41450169) and WhatsApp buttons on mobile, since most buyers want a human to confirm the selection before paying.
- **Pincode delivery check:** On the product page, so a buyer in any city knows if and when it ships, with GST-invoice delivery assurance.
- **Spec comparison:** Side-by-side comparison within a family (for example DPA-370 vs 570M vs 770M, or CSX-3081T vs 5081T vs 6101T) on power, inputs, taps and price, because buyers choose by wattage and channel count.
- **Amplifier-to-speaker wattage matching:** A simple helper that, given a chosen amplifier and 100 V-line speaker taps, shows how many speakers it can drive and suggests a matching set. This is the single most valuable decision-aid for schools, offices, worship and integrators.
- **Clear MRP vs selling price and GST:** Show the saving against MRP and state that prices are GST-inclusive with invoice, since every serious buyer needs the invoice.
- **Set/combo bundles:** "Complete set for shop / classroom / temple" packages (amplifier + speakers + mic + cable) to serve the "give me the full set price" buyer.

## What earns this audience's trust

- **State the authorised-dealer status plainly**, with the Ahuja Radios association clear but positioned as dealer, not manufacturer. "Original / genuine Ahuja" is the single biggest reassurance buyers seek.
- **Genuine product and spec accuracy:** correct model numbers, real wattage and 100 V tap tables, honest photos. Integrators and institutions will not return to a vendor who misquotes a spec.
- **GST invoice and warranty stated up front**, with the manufacturer warranty terms and how service/replacement works.
- **Real contact and a Delhi address:** visible phone numbers, WhatsApp, working hours and a physical shopfront signal a real business, not a drop-shipper.
- **Fast, human response:** a buyer who messages on WhatsApp and gets a correct answer within minutes converts; slow or templated replies lose the sale.
- **Price transparency:** list price with MRP crossed out, GST clarity, and a frictionless path to a bulk/negotiated quote for larger orders.
- **Proof of past work:** installation photos and references for schools, temples and offices build confidence for committee and procurement buyers.

The tone that works is **plain, practical and reassuring in Hinglish-friendly English**: confirm the product is genuine Ahuja, confirm it solves their specific coverage problem, confirm the price includes GST with an invoice, and make a human reachable on phone and WhatsApp in one tap.
